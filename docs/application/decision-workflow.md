# Decision workflow

1. **Intake and evidence:** validate title, proposal, outcome, horizon, scope, nonnegative finite financial inputs and five weights totaling 100. Select current applicable sources, or fail on explicitly selected missing/inapplicable evidence.
2. **Independent assessments:** five distinct structured requests. No initial advisor sees peer conclusions. Record rationale, benefits, risks, assumptions, gaps, citations, gates and scores.
3. **Challenge round:** challenge participants must be different, known advisors. Preserve concerns and responses.
4. **Revision and dissent:** retain originals alongside revised positions; derive changed status from actual output differences. Material objections remain explicit even when there is broad agreement.
5. **Synthesis:** neutral summary, recommendation, agreement and alternatives. The application combines original and revised gates; synthesis cannot discard them. Missing authoritative evidence and policy conflicts block advancement. Verified mission/legal/safety/preservation violations produce a prohibited advisory finding. Review gates yield conditional findings. Scores are informational.
6. **Accountability:** proposed actions, human owners, milestones, dependencies and financial/mission/community/operational measures. Persist the complete report, source metadata, hashes, exact used passages, provider/configuration identity and timestamp atomically with an audit event. Each advisor and facilitator execution also retains its actual start/completion timestamps in the execution trace. Static demonstration timestamps indicate simulation, not model latency.

## Report interpretation

- Verified facts come from approved, dated, authoritative sources with approval evidence.
- Advisor interpretations are rationale and findings; they are not source facts.
- User financial inputs and advisor assumptions are explicitly unverified.
- Recommendations and suggested targets never represent organizational authorization.
- Fictional documents can support a test workflow, never an authentic permissibility finding.

All original safety objections remain in the report even if a revision softens a position. A high score cannot set `eligible_for_human_review` past a blocking gate. The field means conditions are ready for review, not that approval has been obtained. Risk likelihood/impact in mock reports are deliberately unassessed rather than invented numeric probabilities.
